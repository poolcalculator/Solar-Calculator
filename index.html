How the numbers above are calculated

Usage (kWh) = bill ÷ rate. Target generation = usage × your coverage goal. System size (kW) = target generation ÷ (peak sun hours × 30 × 0.78 performance ratio), capped by how many panels actually fit on your roof's South/East/West-facing sections. Roof type adjusts total investment by a flat amount.

What's included in the investment total

Systems over 10 kW AC require $1M liability insurance under Florida's Tier 2 net metering rules (typically $480–$680/year, included above). Battery backup, if selected, is priced separately ($10,000 for the first Powerwall 3, +$7,500 per additional unit) and is included in the total investment and payback period — but not in monthly savings, since Florida's full retail net metering means a battery doesn't reduce your bill further; its value is outage backup. The 25-year projection assumes electricity rates rise 3%/year (Florida's historical average).

What this estimate is — and isn't

This is a pre-sizing estimate meant to help you explore options, not a formal quote or contract. A licensed Florida installer will confirm exact system size, equipment, pricing, and savings after reviewing your roof, shading, structural load, and your utility's interconnection rules — typically with no cost or obligation to you.
